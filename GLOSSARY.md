# EETAA Administrative Changes

Vocabulary for Greek settlement and local authority administrative history published by EETAA (Hellenic Agency for Local Development and Local Government).

## Language

**Settlement**:
An officially recognized Greek populated locality identified by a unique EETAA numerical identifier.
_Avoid_: Village, town, city, locality, place

**Local Authority**:
An officially recognized Greek first-level local government organization (Municipality / Δήμος or Community / Κοινότητα) identified by a unique EETAA numerical identifier.
_Avoid_: OTA, district, council, township, commune

**Authority Type**:
The statutory classification of a local authority as either a Municipality (Δήμος, coded 'Δ') or Community (Κοινότητα, coded 'Κ').
_Avoid_: OTA type, entity category, administration tier, organization kind

**Administrative Change**:
An official statutory act affecting a settlement's or local authority's legal status, name, or administrative attachment, such as annexation, renaming, merger, or transfer, recorded in a Government Gazette issue.
_Avoid_: Mutation, transition, modification, update

**Government Gazette (FEK)**:
The official statutory journal of the Hellenic Republic (Φύλλο Εφημερίδας της Κυβερνήσεως) that legalizes an administrative change, specified by year, issue number, and publication date.
_Avoid_: Decree, legislation, bulletin, gazette issue

**Prefecture (Nomos)**:
The regional administrative unit of Greece to which a settlement or local authority was historically assigned at the time of a specific administrative change.
_Avoid_: County, province, region, department

**Settlement Queue**:
The persistent inventory of known settlements and their discovery and ingestion status, including recorded attempts and their timing.
_Avoid_: Task list, fetch job, work items, download queue

**Local Authority Queue**:
The persistent inventory of known local authorities and their discovery and ingestion status, including recorded attempts and their timing.
_Avoid_: OTA queue, municipality queue, job list

**Name Hint**:
A name associated with an entity in the EETAA discovery index. It is a discovery aid and may differ from names recorded in that entity's historical administrative changes.
_Avoid_: Canonical name, current name, official name

**Change Order**:
The ordinal position of an administrative change in an entity's published change list. It describes source order rather than a chronology inferred from Government Gazette dates.
_Avoid_: Date rank, chronological rank
